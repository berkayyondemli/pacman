# Teo's Spooky Pac-Man

A Halloween take on Pac-Man, made for Teo. You play a glowing jack-o'-lantern
gobbling candy corn in a haunted maze, chased by four ghosts: Vlad, Bubbles,
Slimy and Hazel (the one in the witch hat).

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install or build step.

- **Move:** arrow keys or WASD. On a phone or tablet, swipe or use the arrow pad.
- **Pause:** P or Esc. **Mute:** M.
- **Easy** gives 5 lives, slower ghosts and longer scared time. **Spooky** is closer to the arcade game.

## Scoring

| Thing | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Candy corn | 10 |
| Magic candy (makes ghosts scared) | 50 |
| Scared ghosts in a row | 200, 400, 800, 1600 |
| Candy apple bonus | 100 and up, by level |

You get an extra life at 10,000 points. The high score is saved in your browser.
